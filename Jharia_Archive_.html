About the map and sources

Regional context: the revised Jharia Master Plan was approved on 25 June 2025; a February 2026 Ministry of Coal parliamentary answer describes 81 high-risk sites and reports 1.53 km² of surface fire in the 2024–25 survey. These are official reported figures, not independent validation of our map markers.

For a location-specific claim, we still need an original BCCL/JRDA/NRSC map or document with a place name, date, scale or coordinates, plus a recorded source URL and access date. Until then, Google Maps links are for orientation only.

Ethics, provenance & stewardship

Responsible digital archiving

This archive links to publicly available documents and videos, with credit to their original publishers. Thematic catalogue entries provide an overview of Jharia-related places and subjects; individual entries should be read alongside their original sources when available. Photographs and map locations are approximate visual references, not precise historical boundaries. No private interviews or personal recordings are published. Reuse of third-party content remains subject to copyright and permission, and future personal accounts require informed publication consent. Sources, descriptions and links will need ongoing review, correction and maintenance as the collection develops.
